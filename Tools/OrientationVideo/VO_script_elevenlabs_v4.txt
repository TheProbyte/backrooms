[warm, cheerful 1970s corporate training-film narrator, smooth and confident] Congratulations! You've been hired. Sit down. [short pause] [dry] You're already sitting down. [pleased] Good.

[matter-of-fact, a touch grave] Here's the problem. The world is full. Every shelf... every closet... every storage unit your uncle swore he'd clear out. [firm, final] Full.

[brightening, like unveiling a product] Here's the solution. Behind the world, there's more world. Hallways. Offices. Carpet as far as the eye can see. [conspiratorial] Nobody's using it. [short pause] [proud] So we are.

[salesman charm] Overflow rents that space to people who need it. Storage today. Apartments tomorrow. [friendly, pointing at the viewer] And you, my friend, are how the stuff gets there.

[softer, a little hushed, intrigued] Out there, the walls get thin. We call those seams. Put your ear to one, and you'll hear a warehouse. A print shop. [amused] Somebody's lunch break. [back to brisk] Push a chute in, and the goods come to you.

[brisk, upbeat instructor] Every day, the board has contracts. Store this. Ship that. Furnish these. You read it, you do it, you come back here and collect your pay at the window. [punchy, one word at a time] Read. Do. Collect. [patronising] Even an intern can remember three words. [short pause] [deadpan] Our last intern remembered two.

[businesslike] Office hours. The building keeps them, so you will too. When the closing bell rings, the lights go down, and the building enters what Legal calls its "low-activity period." [slightly faster, a hint of warning] Be back before then. [short pause] [flat, unsettling calm] Low activity is not the same as no activity.

[reassuring, too reassuring] You may notice things have moved. Lights off that you left on. Boxes on the floor. [firm] That is not a ghost. [official, reading from policy] That is a natural phenomenon, and it is not covered by your contract.

[warm, generous] Should you lose consciousness on the job, Overflow will return you to the hub, free of charge. [quick, low aside, almost under the breath] If we can find your body. [cheerful again] Whatever you were carrying stays where you dropped it. Go and get it. [short pause] [pointed] That's company property.

[proud, showing off equipment] This is your work camera. Keep it running. If it isn't on tape, it didn't happen. [leaning in] And if it didn't happen... you don't get paid.

[rousing, slogan voice] Work hard. Work fast. Be back by closing. [short pause] [big, warm sign-off] Welcome to Overflow. [smiling] There's always more room.
